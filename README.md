## Top Digital Asset Management (DAM)

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**



*Focused on Enterprise Digital Asset Management, Brand Asset Organization, Metadata, Workflows & Media Libraries*



**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Asset Management (DAM)**. These systems help organizations store, organize, tag, search, govern, and distribute images, videos, documents, and other digital assets at scale.



**Examples** include Adobe Assets, Bynder, Canto, Brandfolder, Widen Collective, Cloudinary, MediaValet, Frontify, ImageKit, and Acquia DAM (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: While commercial DAM platforms dominate enterprise adoption, strong open-source alternatives exist. **ResourceSpace**, **Pimcore**, **Phraseanet**, **Razuna**, and related projects enable self-hosted, customizable digital asset management with full data ownership. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Adobe Assets / Adobe Experience Manager Assets](https://business.adobe.com/products/experience-manager/assets.html)**  

  Enterprise DAM tightly integrated with Adobe Experience Cloud for managing, delivering, and activating creative assets at scale.



- **[Bynder](https://www.bynder.com/)**  

  Modern cloud DAM platform focused on brand consistency, creative automation, and easy sharing of digital assets across teams.



- **[Canto](https://www.canto.com/)**  

  User-friendly DAM solution for organizing, collaborating on, and distributing digital assets with strong search and portal features.



- **[Brandfolder](https://brandfolder.com/)**  

  Brand-centric DAM platform designed for marketing teams to manage, share, and measure the use of brand assets.



- **[Widen Collective (Acquia)](https://www.acquia.com/products/acquia-dam)**  

  Enterprise DAM (now part of Acquia) offering robust asset management, workflow, and integration capabilities.



- **[Cloudinary](https://cloudinary.com/)**  

  Media management platform with powerful DAM features, AI-based organization, transformation, and optimized delivery of images and video.



- **[MediaValet](https://www.mediavalet.com/)**  

  Cloud-native enterprise DAM focused on security, scalability, and collaboration for large organizations.



- **[Frontify](https://www.frontify.com/)**  

  Brand management platform with strong DAM capabilities for maintaining visual identity, guidelines, and asset libraries.



- **[ImageKit](https://imagekit.io/)**  

  Media management and delivery platform offering DAM features, real-time optimization, and developer-friendly APIs.



- **[Acquia DAM](https://www.acquia.com/products/acquia-dam)**  

  Enterprise digital asset management solution (formerly Widen) integrated into the Acquia digital experience platform.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[ResourceSpace](https://www.resourcespace.com/)** (GitHub: resourcespace/resourcespace)  

  Leading mature open-source Digital Asset Management system. Supports images, video, documents, and audio with powerful metadata, search, permissions, workflows, and API access. Fully self-hostable.



- **[Pimcore](https://github.com/pimcore/pimcore)**  

  Open-core platform combining DAM, PIM, MDM, and digital experience management. Highly flexible for organizations that need asset management tightly linked with product and content data.



- **[Phraseanet](https://github.com/alchemy-fr/Phraseanet)**  

  Open-source DAM platform (GPL) widely used in cultural institutions and enterprises. Features advanced search (Elasticsearch), granular permissions, versioning, and API-first architecture.



- **[Razuna](https://github.com/razuna/razuna)**  

  Open-source digital asset management system with support for multiple file types, metadata, sharing, and both self-hosted and cloud options.



- **[Nuxeo](https://github.com/nuxeo/nuxeo)**  

  Powerful open-source content management platform with strong Digital Asset Management capabilities, workflows, and enterprise scalability.



- **[EnterMedia](https://github.com/entermediadb/entermediadb)**  

  Open-source DAM and media management platform focused on organizing and distributing rich media assets.



- **[Piwigo](https://github.com/Piwigo/Piwigo)**  

  Open-source photo gallery and light DAM solution, excellent for managing and sharing large image collections with plugins and extensions.



- **[Mayan EDMS](https://github.com/mayan-edms/mayan)**  

  Open-source document and electronic management system that can serve as a foundation for asset and document-centric DAM workflows.



- **[Alchemy / related DAM tools](https://github.com/alchemy-fr)**  

  Ecosystem around Phraseanet and other open-source media management components.



- **[PhotoPrism / Immich](https://github.com/photoprism/photoprism)** (complementary)  

  AI-powered self-hosted photo management solutions that can serve lighter DAM or personal/team asset library needs.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Full enterprise-style DAM: **ResourceSpace**, **Phraseanet**, **Pimcore**.

- Flexible / composable platforms: **Pimcore**, **Nuxeo**.

- Lighter or gallery-oriented: **Piwigo**, **PhotoPrism**, **Immich**.

- Accepting that commercial platforms (Bynder, Canto, Adobe Assets, Brandfolder, Cloudinary, Frontify, MediaValet) still lead in polished user experience, AI features, enterprise integrations, support, and scalability for large marketing and brand teams.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Deploy **ResourceSpace** or **Phraseanet** for core DAM functionality → extend with **Pimcore** when you also need PIM/MDM capabilities → complement with AI photo tools like **PhotoPrism** for smarter search and tagging. Ideal for organizations that require data sovereignty, customization, and freedom from per-user SaaS pricing.
